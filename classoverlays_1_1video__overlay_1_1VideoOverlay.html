Detailed Description

Hardware-accelerated rendering container managing localized video rotation loops.

Leverages an optimized QGraphicsView pipeline to guarantee cross-platform alpha channel transparency blending. Manages a static tracking registry to orchestrate mutual audio exclusion states across multi-monitor clinic layouts.
